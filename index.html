Trading stats for any address on Bulk

Paste a Bulk (Solana) wallet address. The site queries the public Bulk API directly from your browser and shows margin, PnL, volume, fees, open positions with liquidation prices, maker/taker split, per-market breakdown, funding, closed-position history, holding time and deposits/withdrawals. No keys or sign-in needed; the address is sent nowhere except the Bulk API.

Challenger Aura comes from a saved snapshot of the public Aura leaderboard (aura.json), because app.bulk.trade does not allow other sites to read it live. The snapshot date is shown on the card.
